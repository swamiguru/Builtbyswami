**Why this exists:** LinkedIn doesn't push articles hard. This post does the work in the feed and sends people to the article. Post the article first, then this, then put the link in the first comment.

**Length:** ~800 characters. The first two lines are all anyone sees before "see more".

---

Everything I built this year was useful and a bit cold. A task manager. A word counter.

So I built one thing that was just nice to sit with.

It plays a city's songs, with that city's sky, at whatever time it actually is there. Delhi at India Gate, golden hour.

Nothing to finish. No number it moves. One afternoon, twenty-four deploys.

It also turned out to be the hardest thing I've made, and not for the reason I expected.

One build failed. Missing dependency, seventeen seconds to fix. That's what failure looks like when there's a test.

The music player took six goes. None of them changed what it plays. Nothing was broken, there was just no test that could pass, so nothing told me to stop. I stopped when I got bored.

I'd written down one rule: one city, do it properly. I shipped three that afternoon. The rule had been holding because building used to be slow, not because I'm disciplined.

Picking the songs took thirty minutes. Building took four hours. The thirty minutes was the only part AI couldn't have done.
